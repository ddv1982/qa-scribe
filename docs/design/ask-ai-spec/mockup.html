Ask AI, one panel everywhere

These mockups go with docs/ask-ai-spec.md. Each panel is drawn at the app's 360px panel width. The Note, Test case and Finding panels share one anatomy: header, source line, conversation and composer. The only differences are the noun, the example prompts, the Finding fields and the Notes footer.

Same panel, three sources (empty state S1)

Opening Ask AI focuses the composer. The example prompts fill the composer and do not send.

One suggestion card: Suggested, then Applied with Undo

The AI never edits by itself. "Update {noun}" applies the change and "Undo" puts it back, the same way in every panel.

Options, working, and the edge states

Everything that isn't the conversation sits behind the sliders icon. Each state replaces one part of the panel and leaves the rest alone.
